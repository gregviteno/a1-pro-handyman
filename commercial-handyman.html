Good To Know

Straight Answers

Do you take recurring maintenance contracts?

Yes. Monthly or quarterly preventative visits keep small problems off your emergency list, filters, hardware, caulking, doors, and a walk-through with a punch list you approve.

Can you handle multiple properties?

Yes, we run multi-unit routes across Westchester, Rockland, Nassau, Putnam, Manhattan, and Fairfield County. One point of contact, per-property invoicing, photos of every completed item.

How fast can you turn over a vacant unit?

A typical patch-paint-hardware turnover takes one to three days depending on condition. Call as soon as you have keys and we'll get you a flat quote and a start date, often the same week.

Are you insured for commercial work?

Yes, licensed and fully insured, and we're happy to provide certificates of insurance for your building or management company before work begins.
