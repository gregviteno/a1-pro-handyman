Why Speed Matters

A Small Hole Never Stays Small

Open drywall invites everything you don't want: moisture, drafts, pests, and a room that never looks finished. And a water stain on the ceiling is a countdown, not a decoration, the longer it sits, the more material has to come out.

Drywall is fast work for a crew that does it every week. Most patches take a single visit including compound, sanding, and primer, so there's no reason to stare at that hole for another month. Call in the morning; by evening the wall is whole again.

Good To Know

Straight Answers

Can you match my wall texture and paint?

Texture, yes, matching the patch to the surrounding finish is the difference between a repair and an eyesore. For paint, we can color-match at the store or use the leftover can in your garage.

Do you repair plaster walls, not just drywall?

Yes. Pre-war Manhattan apartments and older Westchester and Fairfield homes have real plaster, and we repair it properly rather than treating it like drywall.

How long does a typical drywall repair take?

Most patches are done in one visit of one to three hours, including finish work. Large water-damage repairs sometimes need a short second visit after the compound cures.

Can you fix the ceiling after my plumbing leak?

Yes. That's one of our most common calls. Once the leak is stopped (we handle minor plumbing ourselves), we cut out the damaged section, replace it, and finish it paint-ready.
