Good To Know

Straight Answers

My door won't lock, can you come today?

Yes, security issues jump the queue. Call before noon and we can usually repair or replace the lock, latch, or strike plate the same day across our NY and CT service area.

Can you fix drafty windows without replacing them?

Often, yes. Weatherstripping, new sash locks, caulking, and glazing solve most drafts for a fraction of replacement cost. If a window truly is done, we'll tell you honestly.

Do you install doors I've already bought?

Gladly. Slab or prehung, big-box or lumberyard. We'll install it, adjust it, and haul the old one out of your way.

Do you work on old pre-war doors and windows?

Yes. Manhattan co-ops and older homes in Westchester and Fairfield have doors and windows with decades of paint and settling, and coaxing them back into service is a specialty of ours.
