Good To Know

Straight Answers

Can you match my existing trim and molding?

Usually yes. Common profiles we match from stock; unusual ones we can source or come very close. In older Westchester and Fairfield homes, we'll show you the closest match before cutting.

Do you handle exterior rot repair?

Yes, rotted trim, fascia boards we can safely reach, sills, and deck components. Widespread structural rot gets an honest referral instead of a patch that hides the problem.

Is a small carpentry job worth a service call?

That's exactly what we exist for. Bundle two or three items, a loose railing, a chewed-up door frame, a sagging shelf, and the visit more than earns its keep.

Can you build custom shelving?

Yes, small-scope built-ins: closet systems, alcove shelving, garage storage, mudroom benches. We'll sketch it with you, quote it flat, and build it clean.
