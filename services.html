By Service

Installations

Faucets, fixtures, hardware, shelving, and appliances that don't need a licensed trade. If it comes in a box with bad instructions, we install it right.

Home Repair & Upkeep

The running to-do list: squeaks, drips, sticks, wobbles, and gaps. Bundle them into one visit and get the whole page crossed off.

Tile Install & Repair

Backsplashes, cracked tile swaps, regrouting, and small floors. Clean lines, sealed right.

Gutter Cleaning & Repair

Cleaning, re-securing, sealing leaky seams, and downspout fixes on gutters we can safely reach, before the next storm finds the weak spot.

Power Washing

Siding, walkways, patios, and decks brought back to their real color. Great before painting, staining, or selling.

Preventative Maintenance

Seasonal check-ups that catch $50 problems before they become $500 ones: caulk, weatherstripping, hardware, filters, and a punch list you approve.

Aging in Place

Grab bars anchored into structure, handrails, better lighting, threshold ramps, and lever handles, small changes that keep the home safe and livable.

New Homeowner Punch Lists

Just closed? Bring us the inspection report. We knock out the flagged items in one or two visits so the new place starts right.

Selling-Your-Home Fix Lists

Your agent's fix-first list, handled: patches, paint touch-ups, hardware, caulk, and the little flaws buyers photograph. Fast turnaround before listing.

Flooring

Repairs and small installs: creaky boards, damaged plank swaps, transitions, thresholds, vinyl and laminate rooms, and subfloor patches.

Fencing & Decks

Leaning posts, broken pickets, sagging gates, loose deck boards and railings, repaired solid, plus staining and sealing to make it last.

Bathroom Repair & Refresh

Kitchen Repair & Refresh

Bedroom

Living Room

Dining Room

Home Office

Desks assembled, monitors and shelving mounted, cable chaos tamed, and a door that actually closes for calls.

Bathroom Remodel (small-scope)

Kitchen Remodel (small-scope)

Cabinet refresh, hardware, backsplash, faucet, and paint. The 20% of a remodel that makes 80% of the difference.

By Area

Attic

Access doors and ladders, flooring for storage, ventilation fixes, and sealing drafts where the heat escapes.

Deck & Patio

Board and railing repairs, staining and sealing, post caps, and wobbly steps made solid.

Garage

Stairs
