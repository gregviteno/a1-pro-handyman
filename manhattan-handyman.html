Local Answers

Straight Answers

Do you handle co-op and condo building requirements?

Yes, certificates of insurance naming the building, board work rules, service elevator bookings, and approved work hours are routine for us. Send your management company's requirements and we'll have the paperwork in before the visit.

Can you mount a TV on plaster or brick walls?

Yes. Pre-war Manhattan walls are plaster over brick or lath, and standard drywall anchors don't belong anywhere near them. We anchor into the masonry or framing so the mount holds for good.

Which neighborhoods do you cover?

Upper East Side, Upper West Side, Midtown, Chelsea, Harlem, Tribeca, and the Financial District, and Manhattan addresses between them. If you're on the island, call.

Can you work around my building's hours?

Yes. Most Manhattan buildings allow work roughly 9 to 5 on weekdays, and we schedule inside whatever window your building enforces, including freight elevator reservations.
