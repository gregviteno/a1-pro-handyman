Our Story

Why We Built A1 Pro Around Speed

A1 Pro Handyman started with a simple observation: around here, getting a small repair done is strangely hard. Contractors want big jobs. Franchises route you through call centers. Supers have lists a month deep. Meanwhile your door still doesn't lock, and the hole in the wall isn't patching itself.

So we built the company we couldn't find. A family-owned crew that answers its own phone, keeps same-day slots open on purpose, quotes a flat price before starting, and treats a two-hour job with the same care as a two-day one. The person who picks up when you call is part of the same team that pulls up to your house.

We do small-to-mid repairs and improvements, and we're honest about the edges of that: no roofing replacement, no gas lines, no main electrical panel work. Those belong to specialty trades, and we'll tell you so on the phone rather than waste your day.

We live where we work, across Westchester, Rockland, Nassau, and Putnam Counties, Manhattan, and Fairfield County, CT. Our neighbors are our customers, and our name is on every job. That's the whole business model.
