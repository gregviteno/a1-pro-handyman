Good To Know

Straight Answers

How fast can you get here in an emergency?

Call before noon and we can usually reach most of Westchester, Rockland, Putnam, Nassau, Manhattan, and Fairfield County the same day, often within hours. Tell us it's urgent and we'll give you a realistic window on the phone, not a guess.

Do you charge extra for emergency calls?

No surprise markups. You get a flat quote before work starts, same as any other job. Genuine after-hours visits may cost more than a weekday slot, but you'll approve the number first.

My home won't secure after a break-in. Can you help today?

Yes, securing homes is our top priority call. We repair or replace locks, latches, strike plates, and damaged door frames, and we board up broken windows until glass replacement is arranged.

What emergencies do you NOT handle?

Gas leaks, main electrical panel failures, and roof replacement need licensed specialty trades, so call your utility or a specialist for those. We handle the carpentry, drywall, doors, and cleanup work that usually follows.
