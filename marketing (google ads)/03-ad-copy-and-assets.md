# 03 — Ad Copy & Assets

Google Search ads are **Responsive Search Ads (RSAs)**: you provide up to **15 headlines (≤30 chars each)** and **4 descriptions (≤90 chars each)**, and Google mixes them. All copy below is within limits and matches the site's real promises (same-day, call-before-noon, licensed & insured, upfront flat pricing, family-owned, free second opinions).

**Tips:** Pin nothing at first (let Google learn), except optionally pin one location/urgency headline to position 1. Build **2 RSAs per ad group** for testing. Final URLs are set per the landing-page map in `02-campaign-playbook.md`.

## Copy rules to keep it honest & compliant
- **Don't promise guaranteed same-day** — the site says "same-day *when the schedule allows*." Copy mirrors that ("when available," "usually," "on today's schedule").
- Keep "Licensed & Insured," "Family-Owned," "Work Guaranteed" only as long as they're true (they're used site-wide, so they are).
- No fake urgency counters or "only X left" — Google restricts that and it erodes trust with a local repeat-customer base.
