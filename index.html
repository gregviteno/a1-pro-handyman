Straight Answers

Do you really offer same-day handyman service?

Yes. A1 Pro Handyman holds open slots for same-day and last-minute jobs across our New York and Connecticut service area. Call before noon and we can usually have a licensed pro at your door today.

How fast can you get to me?

Often within hours. Our crew is local to the Lower Hudson Valley, Long Island, and NYC, so we're not driving in from three counties away. The earlier in the day you call, the better your window.

What areas do you cover?

We cover Westchester County, Rockland County, Nassau County, and Putnam County in New York, plus Manhattan/NYC and Fairfield County, Connecticut. On the edge of one of those areas? Call anyway. We can usually make it work.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. A1 Pro Handyman is licensed and fully insured for handyman and small construction work in New York and Connecticut. We're glad to share proof of insurance before any job starts.

How does pricing work? Do you charge for estimates?

Estimates are free. Describe the job by phone or send photos, and we give you a flat price before any work begins. You approve the number before we lift a tool.

Is there a minimum job size?

We keep a modest service minimum so the visit is worth your money and ours. Most customers bundle two or three small fixes into one appointment. That's the smart way to do it.

Can I combine multiple small repairs into one visit?

Absolutely. We encourage it. Send us your whole list and we'll quote it as one visit. A leaky faucet, a sticking door, and a wall patch can all get done in a single afternoon.

Do I need to provide tools or materials?

No. Our crew shows up with a fully stocked truck. For materials, we can pick them up for you or install parts you've already bought, whichever you prefer.

Do you handle nights and weekends?

We're open 7 days, and we take evening and weekend appointments when the schedule allows. Emergencies get priority. If something can't wait, call us first.

Do you work with landlords and property managers?

Yes, all the time. Tenant turnovers, punch lists, storefront repairs, and recurring maintenance across multiple units. One call covers it, with photos of the finished work if you're managing from a distance.

What jobs do you NOT do?

We stay in our lane: no roofing replacement, no gas lines, and no main electrical panel work. Those need specialty licenses. Almost everything else on a home's to-do list is fair game, and we'll tell you straight if a job isn't ours.

Why choose a local family crew over a national franchise?

When you call us, you're talking to the crew that shows up. No call center, no dispatch queue, no subcontractor roulette. We live where we work, we can get there faster, and our name is on every job.
